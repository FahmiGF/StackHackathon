2. Problem & Field Evidence (RSUD Kilisuci)

The Core Problem

  • One formula sets every monthly order: 1.10 × Last Month Usage − Stock [1].
  • Rarely used life-saving drugs receive 0 orders after a quiet month.
  • Busy months cause over-ordering & expiration (e.g., Acarbose 100 mg).
  • When budget runs short, funds reach less critical drugs first.

Supporting Evidence

  • Head of Pharmacy [1]: Shortages stem primarily from inaccurate need estimates, secondarily from budget limits.
  • JKN / BPJS [4]: Drugs are bundled; patients cannot be reimbursed for cash purchases outside.
  • e-Katalog [5]: Drug-by-drug purchasing slows emergency recovery [1].
  • Thai & Padang Studies [3][6]: No single formula fits sporadic demand; vital drugs must stay protected.

4. Proposed Solution & Live Demo Flow

Step 1: Upload & Inspect

Upload SIMRS usage/stock + price/DOEN sheets (.xlsx/.csv). Auto-checks columns & suggests DOEN-based VEN flags.

Step 2: Forecast & Adjust

Classifies demand (ADI/CV²), caps stock by Expiry Date, and lets staff override quantities with a logged reason.

Step 3: Rank & Share

Enter monthly budget. Ranks Vital-first, shows expected_days_without_stock, and exports one-page report / CSV.

Scope v1: One pharmacy warehouse, one monthly cycle, zero patient medical records.

6. Pattern Classification & ED-Usable Stock Capping

Syntetos–Boylan (ADI & CV²) + ABC/XYZ/FSN

  • Smooth: Simple Exponential Smoothing (SES)
  • Erratic: Damped Holt Exponential Smoothing
  • Intermittent / Lumpy: SBA (bias-corrected Croston) + Vital Standby Buffer
  • ABC / XYZ / FSN: Quantifies spend, variability, and turnover velocity.

Preventing Expiry Waste (ED Capping)

Usable = min(Physical, Monthly_Forecast × ED_Months) − Loan_Out

Example from RSUD data (Acarbose 100 mg): 4,446 physical units with 6 months to ED → 1,321+ units at expiry risk. SigapStok flags ED risk and sets order to 0.

8. Section 4.4: Vital-First Budget Ranking Logic

  1. Compute Net Order & Runway: Days_of_Supply = Usable_Stock / Daily_Forecast; Expected_Days_Without_Stock = max(0, 30 - DoS).
  2. Lexicographic Priority Tuple: (VEN: V > E > N) → (Lowest Days of Supply) → (ABC-VEN Matrix & Days-Saved-per-IDR).
  3. Constrained Allocation: Assigns FULL_ORDER, PARTIAL_ORDER (for divisible V/E items), or EXCLUDED_OVER_BUDGET with explicit explanation and expected stockout days.
